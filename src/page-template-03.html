01 / The finding

The bigger the price, the less we’re told.

Every one of these fifteen deals is famous for a number, and in almost every case the number is a price. Only five can show what the money changed: Jordan Brand’s sales, Pixar’s purchase price, Fenty Beauty’s first sales, Nestlé’s new coffee products and Amazon’s Prime sign-ups. None has a modelled effect.

The most reliable prices didn’t come from marketing at all. Visa’s FIFA fee and Manchester United’s Chevrolet deal are confirmed in stock market filings, and Google’s $20bn payment to Apple came out in an antitrust trial. Most other values are press estimates, some from a single anonymous source.

When the results stay private, behaviour is the next best evidence. Emirates keeps renewing with Arsenal, and Disney bought the partner it once financed.

05 / The evidence

What a partnership can and can’t prove

Signed, launched, measured

An announcement shows a deal was signed. A launch shows it happened. Only a result shows what it achieved. Each case says which of the three the record supports.

Deal values

A reported price is an input, not an outcome. Where a value appears, it is flagged separately and marked as confirmed, estimated or rumoured.

Two sides, two results

A partnership can work for one side and not the other. Each case lists what each partner brought, and the evidence says which side the outcome belongs to.

Who reported it

Many figures come from the partners themselves or from press estimates. The source for every outcome is named, so you can judge it.

The evidence rating

Every case gets one of five ratings, strongest first: modelled effect, sales trend, survey, reach only and archival. Here a rise in sales or company value counts as a trend. A deal term, such as a stake, is marked so it isn’t read as a result.

How cases earn a place

Each partnership has a known or widely reported value and a public record. Where the value comes from a filing or a court, the case says so. Media credits identify campaign films, archive footage and reporting on the deals.
